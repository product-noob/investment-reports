# US Equity Portfolio & Watchlist — Goal Plan

Started 2026-10-02. Goal: build long-term wealth through disciplined, well-timed US equity decisions.

## How Koda helps
- **Track holdings** — connect brokerage via Plaid to read holdings, balances, and transactions; keep a running view of portfolio performance.
- **Watch the watchlist** — scheduled price and news checks; ping on real moves or when a stock hits your target entry price; quiet otherwise.
- **Research before buying** — earnings, catalysts, valuation, analyst sentiment, what's driving a stock right now.
- **Tracker** — this repo: holdings, watchlist, entry targets, and notes, updated as things change.
- **Reminders** — weekly portfolio summaries, earnings-date alerts, on your cadence.

## Boundaries
- No trade execution, no licensed financial advice. Timing and valuation notes are information and perspective, not recommendations.
